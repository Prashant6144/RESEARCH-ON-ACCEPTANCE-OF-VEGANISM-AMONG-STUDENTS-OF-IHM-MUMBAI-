# README: Acceptance Study of Veganism in First-Year Students of IHM Mumbai

## Overview
This research project explores the awareness, acceptance, and perceptions of veganism among first-year students at the Institute of Hotel Management (IHM), Mumbai. The study investigates dietary preferences, knowledge of vegan health benefits, availability of vegan products, and factors influencing the adoption of a vegan lifestyle.

## Key Findings
- **Awareness**: 60% of students knew about veganism, but 23.5% lacked detailed understanding.  
- **Adoption**: Only 7% followed a vegan diet, while 34.8% identified as vegetarian.  
- **Health Perceptions**: 22.6% believed veganism is healthier, but 48.7% were uncertain.  
- **Accessibility**: 43% were unaware of vegan outlets near their residence.  
- **Experience**: Mixed satisfaction ratings, with 34.8% neutral and 22.6% moderately satisfied.
